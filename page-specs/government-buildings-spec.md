## Unique Angle
Salt Lake City's civic building stock spans 1890s–1960s masonry with aging single-pane windows — a combination of preservation constraints and modern security/energy mandates. GSA compliance, LEED goals, post-9/11 blast standards (UFC 4-010-01), and Utah Act 44 security requirements drive demand. Unlike private commercial buildings, government procurement requires documented standards compliance.
